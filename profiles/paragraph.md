After receiving a PhD in Molecular Biology, Adam switched careers to co-found video game studio
[Butterscotch Shenanigans](https://bscotch.net) ("Bscotch") with his two brothers. At Bscotch he's been variously responsible for a wide variety of things: strategy, business development, contract negotiation, project management, data analytics, and anything else that needed doing. His primary responsibility and passion, however, has always been Productivity Engineering: designing, creating, and maintaining software and processes that boost Bscotch's capacity and efficiency to make and publish games.
